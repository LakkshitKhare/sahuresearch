# Dr. Sushant Prabhakar Sahu — Research Portfolio

> **Official research portfolio website of Dr. Sushant Prabhakar Sahu, Assistant Professor & Ramanujan Faculty Fellow at KIIT Deemed to be University.**

🌐 **Live Website:** https://sahuresearch.vercel.app/

---

## 📌 Overview

The **Dr. Sushant Prabhakar Sahu Research Portfolio** is a modern academic website designed to present the research profile, academic achievements, publications, research interests, projects, and professional activities of **Dr. Sushant Prabhakar Sahu**.

The website serves as a centralized digital profile for showcasing his academic and research contributions, making relevant information accessible to students, researchers, collaborators, institutions, and other academic stakeholders.

## 🚀 Getting Started

